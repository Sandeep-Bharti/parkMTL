Privacy Policy

parkmtl · Last updated September 8, 2026

In one paragraph: parkmtl does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your location is used only on your device to centre the map and is never sent anywhere. If you make a contribution through the app, Apple or Google handle the payment directly — parkmtl never sees your payment details.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the parkmtl mobile app (iOS and Android), published by Sandeep Bharti. It does not cover the third-party websites this app links to (the City of Montréal's open data portal, the Agence de mobilité durable, or OpenStreetMap), which have their own privacy practices.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in of any kind.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software.
  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted to any third party.

Location

If you tap the locate button, the app asks the operating system for your current location, one time, to centre the map on where you are. This uses your device's built-in location services, requires your explicit permission, and can be denied or revoked at any time in your device's Settings without affecting any other part of the app. Your location is never stored, logged, or transmitted — it is used in memory, on your device, for that single purpose, and nothing else.

Parking and map data

The app downloads publicly published parking-regulation data and map tiles from a file hosting service (GitHub Releases) to show and periodically refresh the map. This is a one-way file download: nothing about you or your device is sent as part of it, and no personal data is included in what is downloaded.

In-app purchases

parkmtl offers optional, one-time or monthly contributions to support development. These are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play Store using their own payment systems — parkmtl never receives, stores, or has access to your payment card details. A contribution does not unlock any feature or change how the app works in any way.

Data stored on your device

The app stores the downloaded map/parking data and a small number of app preferences (such as your language choice) locally on your device, using standard iOS/Android app storage. None of this is ever transmitted off your device. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Children's privacy

parkmtl is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, of any age — there is nothing to collect.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new "last updated" date.
